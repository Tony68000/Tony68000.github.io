Tony68000

The personal developer site of Tony Reeves.

Tony68000 is the name I've used for my personal software projects for a long time. The 68000 comes from the Motorola 68000, my all-time favourite processor.

This site is home to the small iOS and Apple Watch apps I've written and published under that name since 2016, along with their support and privacy information.

Apps

  • BinHexDec

    Programmer's calculator · iPhone & iPad · since 2017

    A calculator for working in binary, octal, decimal and hexadecimal side by side, with the bitwise and other operations programmers actually need.

    Details App Store

  • Dice Roller

    Dice simulator · iPhone & Apple Watch · since 2016

    A simple dice roller for iPhone and Apple Watch, for when the real dice have gone missing.

    Details App Store

Current work

BinHexDec is being brought up to date for current versions of iOS. Dice Roller was originally built on the older watchOS app architecture and will be modernised afterwards.

About this site

I write apps, not websites, so this is deliberately a simple static site. I built it quickly with the help of AI tooling, and I'd rather be upfront about that.
