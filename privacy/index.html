Privacy

Privacy information for Tony68000 apps and this website.

Who is responsible

The apps listed on this site, and this website, are developed and published by Tony Reeves (Tony68000).

For privacy questions, email support@tony68000.com.

BinHexDec

Information collected by the app

BinHexDec does not ask you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email address or other contact details. The numbers and calculations you enter stay on your device and are not sent to me.

I do not collect personal information from BinHexDec. The only data that leaves the app is collected by Google's advertising service, as described below.

Advertising and third-party services

BinHexDec is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure how they perform and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app may collect and send to Google information such as:

  • your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, app version and language settings;
  • information about the ads you see and tap;
  • diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data relating to the SDK;
  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking — see below.

Google processes this information under its own terms. For details, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Tracking and personalised ads

On iOS, apps must ask your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before accessing your device's advertising identifier. If you don't allow tracking, BinHexDec still works and ads are still shown, but Google does not receive the advertising identifier and ads are not personalised using it.

You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. You can also manage Google's ad personalisation at My Ad Center.

How information is used and shared

I do not sell your information, and I do not receive the personal data that Google collects through the ads. As the app's developer, I only see aggregated, non-identifying reports from AdMob, such as the number of ads shown and the revenue they earn.

Children

BinHexDec is not directed at children, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Dice Roller

Information collected by the app

Dice Roller does not collect any data. It has no account, does not ask for personal details, and your dice rolls stay on your iPhone and Apple Watch.

Advertising and third-party services

Dice Roller contains no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking services.

How information is used and shared

Because Dice Roller does not collect any information, there is nothing to use or share.

This website

This site is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not use its own cookies or analytics scripts.

As with any website, the hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses when serving pages. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights over your personal information, including the right to ask what is held about you and to have it corrected or deleted. Because I don't hold personal information from either app, most requests about ad data are best directed to Google, using the links in the BinHexDec section above.

If you have any questions or concerns, email support@tony68000.com. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
